Checking Outlook… Sign in to Outlook

Connect your Outlook account

This is a one-time step. Microsoft needs an app registration before any program may send from your mailbox.

  1. Open Microsoft Entra › App registrations and choose New registration.
  2. Name it Email Sender. Under supported account types pick Accounts in any organizational directory and personal Microsoft accounts.
  3. Under Redirect URI pick Public client/native (mobile & desktop) and enter
  4. Press Register, then copy the Application (client) ID into the box below.
